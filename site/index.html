Questions owners ask

Guardrails before growth.

Does this replace our receptionist?

No. DispatchHalo is designed for missed, overflow, and after-hours calls. Your team keeps the daytime relationships; the service covers the gaps and documents the handoff.

Will the assistant give remediation or insurance advice?

No. It follows a written intake policy and fixed safety branches. It does not promise coverage, price, arrival time, claim outcome, or remediation instructions.

What happens if a caller is distressed or the system is unsure?

Low-confidence, negative-sentiment, and policy-trigger calls route to a trained human fallback or an approved client contact. A manual shutdown path is part of the implementation.

Can we keep our current phone number?

Usually, yes. The typical setup uses conditional call forwarding for busy, unanswered, or after-hours calls. The exact method depends on the current carrier.

Is the estimate in the calculator guaranteed?

No. It is a planning model based on the numbers entered and a stated 60% routing assumption. The pilot measures actual calls, qualifications, acknowledgments, bookings, and attributed revenue.
